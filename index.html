About this model

This interactive model shows the post-eruption growth of the lava dome inside Mt. St. Helens crater across three USGS survey epochs: 1982, 1984, and 1986. The mountain base reflects the 1980 crater geometry with a sculpted transition zone and embossed SVG contour elements. The 1952 pre-eruption snowcap is included for reference.

All five mesh objects share a common coordinate space. Toggles let you isolate or overlay any combination of layers, overlapping dome epochs will show Z-fighting, which is expected.

Source data: USGS ScienceBase. Processed in QGIS and modeled in Blender 5.1.2, exported as Draco-compressed glTF 2.0 binary (level 10). For full project context, see the StoryMap.
